# Client interview (consultoria / outsourcing)

## What it evaluates

Common in Brazilian consultancies and outsourcing: after the consultancy approves you, the CLIENT interviews you for allocation. They check autonomy, communication with business people, fast onboarding into their domain and stack, and whether you represent the consultancy well.

## Typical questions

- How do you onboard into a new codebase and domain fast?
- Tell me about working with business stakeholders.
- How do you handle a requirement you disagree with?
- Questions about the client's stack and domain (payments, retail, health…).
- Availability, schedule overlap, sometimes English level.

## Traps

- Not knowing what the client does: research the client, not only the consultancy.
- Discussing salary or contract with the client (that is the consultancy's role).
- Sounding like you will leave at the first better offer.
