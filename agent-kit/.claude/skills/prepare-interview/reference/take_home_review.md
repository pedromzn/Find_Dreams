# Take-home review

## What it evaluates

The reviewer walks through your take-home: decisions, trade-offs, what you would do with more time, how you take criticism.

## Typical questions

- Why this structure / library?
- What would you change for production?
- How did you test it?
- A change request: 'how would you add X?'

## Traps

- Defending every choice instead of acknowledging trade-offs.
- Not remembering your own code (re-read it before).
- Unpaid take-homes over a few hours are a red flag worth noting.
