# Async video interview

## What it evaluates

Recorded answers to fixed questions with a timer (Gupy, HireVue…). Clarity, structure and presence on camera.

## Typical questions

- Tell me about yourself.
- Why this company?
- A behavioral question (a challenge you overcame).

## Traps

- Running out of time: structure each answer in 3 parts.
- Reading from a script on screen.
- Bad light and sound.
