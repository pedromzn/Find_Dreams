# HR screening (triagem de RH)

## What it evaluates

Motivation, communication, salary and contract alignment, availability, work model, culture signals. Brazilian processes often run it through Gupy or a recruiter on WhatsApp; abroad, a recruiter call of 20–30 minutes.

## Typical questions

- Tell me about yourself (the 2-minute pitch).
- Why are you looking for a new job? Why us?
- What is your salary expectation? (CLT vs PJ vs USD contractor)
- When could you start? Notice period?
- Are you OK with hybrid / on-site / relocation?
- Are you in other processes?

## Traps

- Giving a number before knowing the range: ask the range first, then answer with a range adapted to the contract.
- Talking badly about the current employer.
- A pitch that is a CV read aloud instead of a story aimed at this job.
- Saying 'any' when asked about work model or start date and regretting it later.
