# Language interview (English or other)

## What it evaluates

Fluency and clarity for daily work: explaining technical work, small talk, understanding accents. Often a recruiter or a native-speaker engineer.

## Typical questions

- Tell me about yourself / your current project (in English).
- Explain a technical decision to a non-technical person.
- Hypotheticals: 'What would you do if…'
- Small talk about your city, your routine.

## Traps

- Memorized speeches that break at the first follow-up.
- Apologizing for your English all the time.
- Freezing when you miss a word: paraphrase and go on.
