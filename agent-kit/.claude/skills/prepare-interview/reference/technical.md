# Technical interview (especialista)

## What it evaluates

Depth in the stack the job asks for, reasoning, trade-offs, real experience behind the CV lines, honesty about limits.

## Typical questions

- Walk me through a system you built: architecture, your part, what you would change.
- Questions that go one level deeper each time ('why?', 'what happens when…?').
- Concepts of the stack (concurrency, transactions, caching, testing, the framework's lifecycle).
- A production incident you handled.

## Traps

- Vague answers ('we used microservices') without your own decisions.
- Bluffing about a technology the CV does not show: say what you know and how you would learn the rest.
- Not asking clarifying questions.
- Forgetting the 'why' behind choices.
