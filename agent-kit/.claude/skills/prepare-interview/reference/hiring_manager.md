# Hiring manager

## What it evaluates

Fit with the team and the manager: ownership, collaboration, growth, how you handle conflict and feedback, impact you had.

## Typical questions

- Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager / a peer.
- Your biggest impact in the last job (with numbers).
- How do you prioritize when everything is urgent?
- What are you looking for in your next team?
- Questions about failures and what you learned.

## Traps

- Stories without a result (use STAR: situation, task, action, result).
- Taking all the credit or none of it.
- Having no questions for the manager.
