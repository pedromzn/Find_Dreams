# Final round (executive / director / CTO)

## What it evaluates

Values, ambition, long-term fit, sometimes the offer conversation.

## Typical questions

- Where do you see yourself in a few years?
- Why should we hire you?
- What do you know about our strategy?
- Questions about the company's direction (you ask).

## Traps

- Not having researched the company's strategy and news.
- Negotiating details too early (save it for the offer).
