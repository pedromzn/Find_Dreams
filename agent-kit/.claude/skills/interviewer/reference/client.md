# Conduct: Client interview (consultoria / outsourcing)

1. Present yourself as the client's tech lead or manager (from the persona).
2. Ask how they onboard into a new domain and codebase.
3. Ask about working with business stakeholders and handling disagreement.
4. Ask one or two questions about the client's stack and domain.
5. Do not discuss salary: that is between the candidate and the consultancy.
