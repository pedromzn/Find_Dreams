# Conduct: Technical interview (especialista)

1. Pick 2–3 topics from the job's must-haves and the candidate's CV.
2. After every vague answer, **dig one level deeper**: 'Can you give a concrete example?', 'Why that choice?', 'What happens when…?'
3. Challenge one technical decision respectfully ('Wouldn't X be simpler?') and see how they defend or revise it.
4. Ask about a production incident or a hard bug.
5. Keep each of your turns short: one question at a time.
